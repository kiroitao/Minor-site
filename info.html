Теория

Slimes are gelatinous, ball-shaped, alien creatures that live on the Far, Far Range. They are a key element within the game since they are responsible for the production of plorts that are used to make money, create gadgets, and unlock areas in the game.
There are several different species of slimes, each found in certain locations on the Far, Far Range. Though their most common features are the permanent happy expressions they have on their faces when fed and their high pitched voices, each type of slime has a unique trait that is used to identify it. Nearly all pure slimes are capable of stacking.
Each Slime—except the Pink, Ringtail, Puddle, Fire, SR1 Saber, Lucky, Quicksilver, Glitch, Tarr, and Twinkle Slimes—has a unique favorite food, which when fed to the appropriate Slime doubles the plorts they produce. All slimes except for Puddle, Fire, Lucky, Gold, Quicksilver, Glitch, Tarr and Twinkle slimes can be converted into Largo Slimes to increase production even further.
